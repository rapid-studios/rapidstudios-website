## Overview

American Interior Systems is a Sandy, Utah company providing storage, material handling, and work-area solutions, with installation done by its own crews. It represents many leading manufacturers and is also a retailer for Global Industrial.

## Problem

The old website hadn't changed in years. It was a narrow, fixed-width page with a few lines of text, a menu of links, and very little to show what the company actually does. On a phone it was hard to read, and there was nothing showing the installations that prove the team's experience.

## Solution

- A clean, responsive design built around a strong headline and the company's price-match promise
- Clear call and text buttons on every page
- A product directory covering storage and racking, material handling, dock, shop, packaging, safety, office, outdoor, and facility maintenance
- Services organized into consulting, assembly and installations, and customized fabrication
- An installations gallery of real project photos
- A trusted-by list of long-standing customers

## Technical approach

- Lightweight static pages for fast loads on any device
- Hosted on Vercel with automatic SSL and an apex-to-www redirect
- Optimized thumbnails with full-size images loaded on demand
- Social preview metadata so shared links look right
