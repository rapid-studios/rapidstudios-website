## Overview

Sant Electric is a licensed electrical contractor based in Eagle Mountain, Utah, doing residential, commercial, and new-construction work across the Wasatch Front. The business runs its jobs in JobTread, and it needed a website that looked as professional as its crews and fed new work directly into that system.

## Problem

The existing Wix site was built in the editor and hard to keep current. Project photos weren't telling the story of the work, and the quote form gave customers no help figuring out what to ask for. The owner wanted something that looked custom without having to learn a site builder.

## Solution

- A fully custom design built in Astro and served through Wix Headless, so the business keeps its existing Wix account, domain, and plan
- JobTread web forms for quotes, contact, and hiring, wired so each submission creates a customer and a job automatically
- SantBot, a guided quote assistant that wraps the real JobTread form, with photo uploads for the job site
- Project galleries organized by job, with lightbox viewing
- Search groundwork: structured data, a clean sitemap, and 301 redirects from the old site's URLs

## Ongoing care

The site is covered by a monthly care plan. A daily health check and five-minute uptime monitors watch every page, every form embed, and every script and image the site depends on, so issues are caught and fixed before they cost a lead.
