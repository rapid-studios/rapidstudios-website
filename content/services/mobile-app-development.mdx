## What this covers

We start with a specific customer task and agree which screens, features, and integrations belong in the first release. Production development, testing, and submission support are scoped around your business systems and the access available to them.

Developer accounts, third-party services, and supported integration access may be needed. App Store and Google Play approval and review timing are controlled by the stores. A preview or prototype demonstrates a direction; production app development and submission are separate deliverables in the agreed scope.
